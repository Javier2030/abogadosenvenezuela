Partner rates, not commissions

When you are our client — you sign the engagement and pay our invoice — you get partner rates from your second engagement, set out in a written partner agreement. The saving is on your own invoice.

We do not pay referral fees or commissions to anyone. The Code of Professional Ethics of Venezuelan lawyers (art. 12) treats rewarding those who recommend clients as a serious breach, and we prefer to say so up front.

Compliance

  • Before quoting, we identify every shareholder and ultimate beneficial owner and screen them against the OFAC and EU sanctions lists.
  • We decline cases involving sanctioned persons, counterparties linked to PDVSA, or mining and gold.
  • We advise on Venezuelan law. US sanctions are US law: if your client's project depends on an OFAC license, it needs US sanctions counsel.
  • Sanitary registrations, hydrocarbons licenses and bank account approval depend on third parties; we never promise them as a result.

Timelines: what depends on us and what does not

  • Our work is completed within one month; the Phase 0 opinion takes 5 business days.
  • Pre-registration of foreign shareholders at SAREN has been reported to take from two weeks to several months; incorporation then takes 5–10 business days.
  • Signing at the registry is in person, with biometric identification since SAREN Providencia 525 (Official Gazette 42.987, October 2024); the signer can be your client's attorney-in-fact in Venezuela.
  • Municipal license: about 30 business days. Visas and bank accounts depend on the consulate, the passport and the bank; before quoting we tell you which nationalities take longer.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pay referral commissions?

No. Venezuelan rules of professional ethics for lawyers do not allow rewarding those who refer clients. Partners who contract and pay us directly get partner rates from their second engagement instead.

Can you work under our brand?

Yes. We report to you in English and you present the work to your client. Documents filed with Venezuelan authorities carry the name of the Venezuelan lawyer who signs them, as the law requires.

Who signs the engagement?

You do, as our client. If your client prefers to contract with us directly, they become our client and partner rates do not apply.

Does the end client need to travel to Venezuela?

In most cases, no: a power of attorney with apostille or consular legalization lets a Venezuelan attorney act. The work visa may require a visit to a consulate.

How are you paid?

Each phase is paid when it begins, by international bank transfer or money transfer, or in USDT, as set out in the written contract.

Which nationalities do you accept?

Any, after screening. Some nationalities take longer at banks and consulates, and we say so before quoting. We decline sanctioned persons.
