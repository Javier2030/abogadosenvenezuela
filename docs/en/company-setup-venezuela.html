Why now

Venezuela is reopening to foreign trade in 2026: direct flights with the United States and Europe are back, energy companies are returning, and day-to-day business already runs largely on US dollars. What you should know before you start

  • Foreigners can own and run a company in Venezuela. Some activities are reserved or regulated (road transport of people and cargo, hydrocarbons, mining, banking, media); if yours is one of them, we design the structure so that it can operate legally.
  • Registering a foreign investment with the State requires a minimum of about €800,000. Below that you can still incorporate and operate without problems; we explain how to structure your capital so that you can bring your money back.
  • Work visa requirements depend on your passport, and Venezuelan banks review some nationalities more strictly. We tell you this before you pay, not after.

Frequently asked questions

Can a foreigner own a company in Venezuela?

Yes. A foreign national can own and manage a Venezuelan company. Only some activities are reserved or regulated, and in those cases we design a structure that allows the business to operate legally.

Do I need to travel to Venezuela?

In most cases, no. You sign a power of attorney in your country, with apostille or consular legalization depending on the country, and we act on your behalf. For the work visa you will need to deal with the consulate or travel at the stage we indicate.

How long does it take?

Our work is completed within one month. Government offices and the consulate have their own response times, which we cannot control; we tell you in advance which steps can take longer.

How much capital do I need?

There is no legal minimum capital for a company. About 3% of the capital you declare is paid in registration fees, so the right amount depends on your plan; we define it in the structure opinion.

Can I get a work visa?

Yes. The package includes one work visa (two in the Investor package). The requirements depend on your nationality, and we check them before you commit.

How do I pay, and in which language do you work?

By phases: each phase is paid when it begins, starting with Phase 0 (US$600). In USDT through Binance, or in Venezuela or Colombia. We work in Spanish and English; you can write to us in your own language.
